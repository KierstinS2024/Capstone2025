# #User Flow & Story-Driven Walkthrough

![REVISED_UserFlowDiagram](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/db6c3b87-e505-4975-9f42-0f6f41bd5676)

## [1] Landing Page

- Explore as Guest: view sample recipes (read-only), preview meal planner

- Sign Up: email, password, dietary preferences, Google sign-up option, confirmation email

- Log In: email/password or Google, redirect to Dashboard

## [2] User Dashboard

- Overview Panel: today's meals, pending groceries, nutrition summary, notifications

- Profile: edit dietary preferences, upload/change photo

- Create New Meal Plan: Meal Plan Wizard, drag-and-drop, serving adjustments, save plan

- View Existing Meal Plans: edit, delete, clone

- Generate Shopping List: ingredients grouped by category, editable, print/export

- Search Recipes: filter by ingredient, diet, cuisine

- Saved Recipes: add your own recipe with nutritional info

- Track Nutrition (Food Intake): log meals, autofill nutrition

- Settings: password, notifications, dark mode toggle

- Log Out

## [3] Recipe Details Page

- Ingredients list, step-by-step instructions, cooking mode

- Nutritional info (calories, macros)

- Save to profile / add to meal plan

## [4] Meal Plan Wizard

- Select week (Mon--Sun), pick meals for each day

- AI-based recommendations based on profile

- Adjust serving sizes, save plan → Dashboard

## [5] Generate Shopping List

- Based on selected meal plan

- Group ingredients by category, editable quantities

- Mark purchased, print/export

## [6] Nutrition Tracker

- Log meals consumed, autofill nutrition info

- Daily nutrition summary (calories, protein, carbs, fats)

- Weekly/monthly progress trends with charts

- Set/update custom nutrition goals

# 📖 Story-Driven Walkthrough

### Chapter 1: The Discovery -- Landing Page

Sarah explores as a guest, browses sample recipes, and previews the meal planner. Later, she signs up with email/password and dietary preferences, verifies her account, and logs in to the dashboard.

### Chapter 2: Personalized Space -- User Dashboard

Sarah views her day's meals, grocery reminders, and nutrition summary. She edits dietary preferences, updates profile photo, and adjusts notification settings.

### Chapter 3: Building the Perfect Plan -- Meal Plan Wizard

Sarah launches the Meal Plan Wizard, selects meals for each day, drags-and-drops, adjusts portion sizes, and saves the plan.

### Chapter 4: Grocery Shopping Made Easy -- Shopping List

Sarah generates a shopping list from her plan, grouped by category, edits quantities, marks purchased items, and exports as PDF.

### Chapter 5: Discovering and Creating Recipes

Sarah searches recipes, adds her own with ingredients, steps, and nutritional info. Her recipe is saved for future meal planning.

### Chapter 6: Cooking and Tracking -- Recipe Details & Nutrition Tracker

Sarah follows recipe steps, tracks meals consumed, and checks daily nutrition summary with autofilled macros.

### Chapter 7: Revisiting and Refining -- Saved Recipes & Meal Plans

Sarah revisits saved recipes, adds favorites to upcoming plans, and edits existing plans as needed.

### Chapter 8: Customizing Experience -- Settings & Preferences

Sarah updates password, notification preferences, and toggles dark mode for a personalized experience.

### Chapter 9: Reaching Her Goals -- Nutrition Tracker

Sarah views weekly/monthly trends, compares intake to goals, and sets new nutrition targets.

### Chapter 10: The Journey Continues

Sarah is fully engaged: planning meals, shopping, tracking nutrition, and using personalized recommendations to maintain healthy habits.

**End of Story.**
